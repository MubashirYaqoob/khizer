# Al-Huda Fabric - Frontend Specification

## Project Overview
A premium eCommerce platform for Al-Huda Fabric, specializing in Stitched, Unstitched, and Customized Pakistani suits.

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Al-Huda Fabric
- **Style:** Luxury minimal, fast-loading, light background.
- **Colors:** White, Cream (#faf8f5), Black (#1a1a1a), Gold (#b8943f).
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond (Serif), Montserrat (Sans-serif).

## Pages & Features
1.  **Homepage:** Hero, Categories (Stitched, Unstitched, Customized), Featured Products, Custom Order CTA, Footer.
2.  **Category Page:** Grid view with filters (Size, Price).
3.  **Product Detail:** Gallery, Size Chart Modal, Add to Cart.
4.  **Customized Suit Page:** Detailed measurement form + fabric selection.
5.  **Cart:** Line items, totals.
6.  **Checkout:** Customer info, COD/Online payment options.
7.  **Order Confirmation:** Success message + summary.
8.  **Gallery:** Masonry grid of lifestyle images.
9.  **About Us:** Brand story.
10. **FAQ:** Toggle-style questions.
11. **Legal:** Privacy & Terms.
12. **Auth:** Login / Register.

## Key Components
- Navbar: Search, Wishlist, Cart.
- Product Card: Image, Name, Price, Sale Badge.
- WhatsApp: Floating action button.
- SEO: Meta tags on every page.
- Tech: Next.js 14 structure, Tailwind CSS.
