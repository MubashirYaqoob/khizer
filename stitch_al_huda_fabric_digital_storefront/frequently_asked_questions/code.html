Shipping Information

We offer global shipping to over 150 countries. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the weight of the parcel and the destination. For orders over $300, we offer complimentary international shipping via DHL Express.

Standard delivery within Pakistan takes 3-5 business days for major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad). Remote areas may take up to 7 business days. During sale periods or new collection launches, processing may take an additional 2-3 days.

Yes. Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with a unique tracking number and a link to the courier's portal. You can also view status updates in your "Order History" under the profile section.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept exchanges within 14 days of delivery if the item is in its original, unworn condition with all tags intact. Please note that sale items and customized stitched outfits are non-exchangeable unless there is a manufacturing defect.

For exchanges due to preference, the customer is responsible for shipping costs. If you received a damaged or incorrect item, Al-Huda Fabric will cover all courier expenses to facilitate the return and replacement.

Sizing & Fit Guide

Our sizing follows international standards but with a tailored fit. We provide a detailed measurement chart on every product page. Key measurements to check are:

  • Chest/Bust across the fullest part
  • Shoulder width from bone to bone
  • Kameez length from shoulder to hem

Custom Orders

Yes, we offer premium "Stitched to Order" services. You can select standard sizes (XS-XL) or provide your custom measurements. Please allow an additional 10-14 business days for artisanal tailoring before dispatch.
