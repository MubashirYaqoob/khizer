At Al-Huda Fabric, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our customers. This section outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you interact with our brand.

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details. This information is essential for processing orders and providing a personalized shopping experience.

We strive to deliver our premium fabrics with the utmost care. Please note the following regarding our delivery process:
